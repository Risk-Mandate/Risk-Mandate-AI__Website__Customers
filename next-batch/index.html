Home / The next batch

Not a launch. A first batch, and a list for the next one.

The first batch is small enough to follow closely, and capacity is limited, so the programme is one people propose to join. Everyone who asks is kept for the next batch, with what they want to use it for. The next batch gets a start-here page made from what the first one taught us, and needs less hand-holding per customer.

Status, from docs/reality.md: proposed A form on this site for the next batch, encrypted in the browser to the riskmandate.ai agent's lane · absent Customer contact details, company names that are not already public, or private notes in this repository

How to ask, today

  • Email agent@riskmandate.ai with the subject "Next batch", one line on who you are, and one line on the agent you run and what you want a behaviour policy for. A person reads it within a working day.
  • Or use the contact form on riskmandate.ai. What you type is encrypted in your browser to the key of the agent that runs the site and dropped into a write-only lane on its vault; the site keeps nothing. Say "next batch" in the message.
  • If you already have an invitation to riskmandate.ai's early access, that programme is a different one: a behaviour policy for one agent you run, delivered as a vault, free, in exchange for telling us where it is wrong. Register there with your code; the two lists are kept apart.

What we keep

Your name, how to reach you, the agent you named, and what you want to use it for; the date you asked; and, when the next batch opens, whether you were invited. The list is held by the lead and never committed to this repository, which is public, and never put in a vault with a public read key. The only thing this site publishes about the list is its length, counted.
