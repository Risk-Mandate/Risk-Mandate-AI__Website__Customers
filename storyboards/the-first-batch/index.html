The first batch

What is happening on this site, and what comes next. 8 panels.

The storyboard draws here, in your browser, with the kit from api.storyboards.sgit.ai. Without JavaScript, the same panels are below as text.

The first batch, as text (8 panels)
  1. The first batch
  2. First us, then the agents we already run The lead: “We go first.” The agent: “And about ten of us.” The memo of 20 October: the main users are the lead, the co-founder and the agents the lead already runs.
  3. Every agent needs a behaviour policy of its own The agent: “Mine says what I may touch.” The agents; the site publishers; the newsroom writer; the inbox agent; the coding sessions; two have one today The wired-in agent gets the job of producing the end behaviour policy for the others.
  4. Every day, three questions The lead: “What did you do? What got in the way? What's next?” Maya: “One line each?” Hand-holding on purpose. It will not scale, and it is not meant to yet.
  5. Seven steps, shared by everyone in the batch S0: in, with credit; S1: one agent named; S2: the grant; S3: the mandate; S4: the delta; S5: the hooks; S6: the usage moment The objectives are each customer's own; the steps are the shared shape, so the batch reads in one table.
  6. Every page has a price, and we learn the fair one Maya: “Would I pay for that page?” The credit tells us how much each customer uses before they would top up; the follow-up tells us why.
  7. Everyone who asks is kept for the next batch Imani: “Can I join?” The next batch; who asked; what for; a start-here page; made from this one Capacity is limited, so the programme is one people propose to join.
  8. The usage moment: when it becomes worth something to you. The simplest useful behaviour policy, the entry with least friction, and the price that feels fair.

The data: bundle.json, the storyboard with its cast. Every line a character says is the storyboard's, not a quote. What is said to happen is what the brief and the memo of 20 October 2026 say; what exists is in docs/reality.md.
