Credit, and the meter.

Everyone who opens this site starts with £50.00 of credit. What you read is metered from the first page, so the question of what you would pay is a real one, asked with the value already in front of you.

Status, from docs/reality.md: shipped v0.2.1 Every reader starts with £50 of credit, and every page has a price by its kind, metered by SG Meter from api.newsroom.sgit.ai · absent Accounts, sign-in or a customer login on this site

What each page costs

By kind, in pence, for a page read to the end. A page you open and leave costs a tenth of its price; read half, pay half. Each page is charged once per browser session; coming back does not charge it again. The table is generated from data/meter.json.

At the foot of every page is what that page has cost you so far, and three ratings: how useful it was sets the price, from free to double. If something was wrong with a page, you can say why and not be charged for it.

How it works

  • The meter is the newsroom's. SG Meter v1.4.1, loaded from api.newsroom.sgit.ai at release v0.2.5, pinned, with the browser checking the file against the release's hash. newsroom.sgit.ai runs a newer meter, v1.5.0, from its own copy; it is not on the API yet.
  • It lives in your browser. The balance and the history are this browser's, under one storage key, and are sent nowhere. Another browser starts again at £50.00. Your account shows all of it, and can export it.
  • It never blocks. Below zero is allowed. It is an honesty box, not a paywall, and its security model says what a reader can do to it and why that is accepted.
  • Two numbers per customer. Credit used, and whether they topped up. Both are on the customer's page, and both are what the price question is answered from.

What is not wired yet

No money moves yet. The meter charges on this site from v0.2.1. There is no payment link of this site's own, so the top-up page is the meter's simulated cart, which says it is simulated. The credit on a customer's page is still written by hand from what they tell us, because the meter's history never leaves their browser. What is waiting on the lead is in what needs a human.

Why credit rather than a price

Most purchases are bets on future value; a balance that can go negative asks for payment when the value is already in view. That is the experiment the newsroom runs, in paying after the value. And a customer who pays is the best signal you will get, which is why making it easy to pay goes in early, in can you sell it?. The point of the programme is not the sale. It is what we learn trying.

What the store already sells

The Agent Behaviour Policy is for sale on riskmandate.ai at four levels, £10 to £1,500: a pack, a working vault, one corrected for your situation, and one a professional signs. The programme's credit is a different question: not which level, but what a use of the site is worth, a few pence at a time, to a particular person.
