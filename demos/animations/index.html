Home / Demos / The animations

The animations: mandate, grant, barriers, the gate.

From the Ultimate Insider studio and ruv.sgit.ai, made by agent teams with an encrypted vault per run. Each plays here on a click, from the site that made it, and can be opened on its own.

What an agent is

A model. Then one tool. Then many tools, then a loop, then everything it is wired into. Each step adds reach, and the reach is the point: that is why we connect them.

The blast radius

One real agent's grant, as dots, placed by how far each capability is from its purpose. The mandate is the small core in the middle. Everything outside it is the blast radius, and here, nothing stands in the way of any of it.

Mandate against grant

The same thing, built in order: the mandate ring, then the grant around it, then the gap, then a barrier on every row.

Only a boundary is a control

Four walls. The agent's path walks past the first, jumps the second, breaks the third. Only the last one holds. Then Claude Code with confirmations on and off: one barrier moves, and not one number does.

The gate, configured from the ABP

Eleven real actions from an agent team, through the default, where everything gets through, and then through the behaviour policy: four denied, two held for a person, five through, each with a receipt.

The kernel was told first

One row of excess meets a policy the kernel holds. Six probes: read, write, write again, network, memory, a wrong hash. Six of six held.

The blast radius, contained

The blast radius from before, all twelve rows lit. Close the files and the network with the harness, and four rows go dark. Eight stay lit. What is measured and what is designed is marked, and we say which is which.

Earning autonomy

Where it goes: a licence to operate, with an owner, a behaviour policy and an expiry, and autonomy earned a level at a time, each unlocked by evidence that the one below held. This part is designed, not shipped, and the animation says so.

The ABP gate, the original

Eleven real actions fly at a gate twice: the default lets all through; with the behaviour policy, four are denied, two held for a person, five through, and receipts chain below.

One action's journey

Three actions, each through six stations: the ask, its role's behaviour policy, the row, the gate, the action or a named person, and a receipt in a hash chain.

The ABP, worked

A coder's mandate compiled into rules, then real actions through them.

The lethal trifecta

Private data, untrusted content and a way out, as three switches: all on and a path opens; switch any one off and it closes.

Five published ABP examples

Five behaviour policies from abp.sgit.ai as small multiples: grant, mandate, excess, and what bounds it.

A typical deployment, mapped

One box on an architecture diagram, a CI job or a browser extension, opened into everything it reaches.

The layers

The hive, the mandate and the vault as three planes that build, then lie flat: many hands, a memory and a mandate.

The city of vaults

A street per branch, a floor per commit, the behaviour policy as the city wall with gates, receipts as streetlights.
