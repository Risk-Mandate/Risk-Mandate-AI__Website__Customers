Release history

One row per release, newest first. The version lives in admin/build/version.txt, goes up on every push to dev, and is the commit subject, site vX.Y.Z : what. CI tags the commit and deploys it, and then checks the live host serves it. The note says what changed and why, not "UI improvements".

v0.2.52026-10-21the first run in a real browser, written up and replayed: run C1 beside a flow chart and a storyboard, the brief for browser agents, and what it found fixedRun C1: an agent in Chrome on the lead's iMac played Priya, an invented operations lead, through all six examples in real Claude and sent back 40 screenshots and a report, kept as it came. Our structured record of it (run.json) holds every step: what was on screen, what it means, and for each of Claude's 21 answers what the page said beside what Claude wrote (15 matched, 4 in part, 2 were moments the page never showed). The article at /feedback/runs/C1/: the run replayed by a new player (assets/replay.js) with the demos' controls, a pace, a flow chart and a scorecard that follow it, replays of each example, what worked, every finding checked and mapped to the register, the one sentence that switched a line off, and what this workflow means. An experiment at /feedback/runs/C1/storyboard/: the five real steps beside a new six-panel storyboard, One sentence, which turns with them. For the next runs: /feedback/runs/brief/ (what to give a browser agent, with what to expect and think at every step, how to take screenshots, and run.json in the shape the replays read) and /feedback/runs/personas/. A player no longer starts by itself after the reader has used its controls. R004 recorded in 11 points; the register has 60 proposals.
v0.2.42026-10-21Encrypt and send to the agent, the big account, a player that waits for you, and Reviews in the menu; R003 read point by pointR003, the lead's third memo, kept verbatim and read in 12 points with screenshots of v0.2.3. Encrypt and send: answers and messages are encrypted in the browser (sgit's hybrid envelope v2) and appended to the feedback lane of an encrypted vault made in this session, zs81fic5 on dev.send.sgraph.ai; the public key and append token are in feedback/lane.json (the leak check allows that one token in that one file), only the vault's key can read what arrives, and admin/feedback/lane.mjs configures, tests and drains it; tested end to end from Node and from a real browser. How this works is a page of its own; email only if sending fails; Copy kept; a box on /feedback/ to send anything; agents can send the same way. The account shows everything a reader did and everything kept in their browser: balance, five counts, each example's progress, answers and sends, where the credit went, everything read, top-ups, and every stored item, with Export everything and Delete everything. The player starts only after you have scrolled to it, shows a bar of the steps by act, counts down before each new act, keeps playing on Back and Next, and says when it has ended. Reviews is a menu of its own, the home page has a band of the latest reviews, and every footer links to them; the menu folds at 1260px. The card label that wrapped is fixed. The register has 46 proposals.
v0.2.32026-10-21web first: six examples to try in Claude or ChatGPT, played step by step, with questions under each; no hooks on the front pages; an account page of our own; R002 read point by pointR002, the lead's second memo, kept verbatim and read in 26 points with screenshots of v0.2.2. The home page and start here are now in the browser: open Claude or ChatGPT, ask, paste one line, ask again, new chat with the line first. Six examples in tabs, simplest first (hello world, stay on topic, no web search, keep a secret, drafts never send, refunds up to 50 pounds), each versioned, each in a chat frame drawn like Claude's or ChatGPT's, each with the prompts to copy and five questions whose answers the reader emails. Every demo on the site can be played, paused, stepped back and forward. No hook and nothing to install on the front pages; the Claude Code behaviour policies are kept further down /policies/ under a warning. The account strip sits under the hero; the account page is our own view of the meter, without the newsroom's personas; the meter's reader card under each page is gone, and it still charges. Cards show pictures of where they go. The register has 37 proposals; D1 is recorded as declined at v0.2.2 and reversed at v0.2.3, with why. Questions Q4 to Q6 for the lead.
v0.2.22026-10-21the feedback loop: the lead's first review read point by point with screenshots, five invented users run through v0.2.1, and one register of 23 proposals each with a decision and a reasonR001, Dinis Cruz's first impression, kept verbatim with our reading point by point and screenshots of v0.2.1: the hook answers a question nobody has asked yet. Study S1: five invented users (Sam, Nadia, Marcus, Joan, Tomas) drove v0.2.1 one screenshot at a time; 29 findings, each checked (24 confirmed, 4 partly, 1 artefact). /feedback/: one line by email, a voice memo your Claude turns into a review, or a Claude interview. v0.2.12026-10-21the customer first: £50 of credit metered by SG Meter, six behaviour policies with tested hooks, storyboards and demosThe home page is now the customer's: £50.00 of credit, one worry, one line, one hook, one prompt to try it, with the programme's logistics moved to the programme pages. Every page has a price by its kind (1p the home page, 2p a page, 10p a storyboard, 50p a behaviour policy, £1.00 the starter pack), charged by how far it is read by SG Meter v1.4.1 from api.newsroom.sgit.ai release v0.2.5, pinned and loaded with its manifest's hash; nothing is blocked and there is no payment link yet, so top-ups are simulated and say so. Six behaviour policies from riskmandate.ai's rules: four for Claude Code with a line and a PreToolUse hook (secrets, pushes, deletes, its own permissions), each hook tested with an allowed twin for every refusal; two for ChatGPT and Claude with a line, said to be an expectation only; and a starter pack Claude Code installs from one prompt, showing every file first. Three storyboards drawn by the kit from api.storyboards.sgit.ai v0.1.0, loaded with integrity, its delay attributes allowed by hash and nothing else inline; three demos of a session on a drawn laptop or browser, labelled as illustrations. The header fits every width from 320 to 1440 pixels with the balance in it. From this release every push moves the third number.
v0.2.02026-10-21six themes, the newsroom's five mapped onto RiskMandate's look plus its own dark; the header fits an iPadA theme pill in the header picks one of six, kept in this browser: RiskMandate (the default, riskmandate.ai's paper and green), Canvas (its dark, from the dark hero band), and newsroom.sgit.ai's Night, Ember, Paper and Blueprint, mapped onto this site's tokens. Every colour now lives in assets/themes.css and nowhere else; site.css reads tokens only. A new test fails the build on a theme missing a token, a colour written outside that file, or a pair under its contrast in any theme; it found riskmandate.ai's green at 4.24:1 on its own alternate band, so the link accent is one shade darker. The header lost the first-batch stage pill, gained the picker beside the version, and now folds its links behind the menu button below 1100px: between 1040 and 1200 the version chip used to run past the right edge and the page scrolled sideways, which is an iPad in landscape. Wide tables scroll inside themselves on a phone instead of widening the customers page. docs/ops/themes.md says how it works and how to add a seventh.
v0.1.12026-10-20the repository moved to Risk-Mandate; the source links and the DNS target follow itThe lead moved the repository from the SGit-AI organisation to Risk-Mandate/Risk-Mandate-AI__Website__Customers between the first push and the first CI run, and pointed customers.riskmandate.ai at risk-mandate.github.io. Every source link on the site, the release script's Actions lookup, the participant disclosure and the DNS line in needs.md now name the new home; the corrections file records the move as C9. Nothing else changed.
v0.1.02026-10-20the first customers site: the brief, the start-here page, the batch, the steps and the gateThe site exists, as customers.riskmandate.ai, a sub-site of riskmandate.ai, built from the newsroom brief of 20 October 2026 and the voice memo that followed it the same day. What is here: the brief published in full with its corrections file beside it; a start-here page in order; the programme pages (customers not beta users, credit and the meter, the seven milestone steps, the daily follow-up, what we want to discover, the next batch); a page per customer generated from data/customers.json with no contact details in it; the reality file; the team as files; the review folder with the brief as intent; and the eight-check gate with a leak tripwire and the house words. What is not here: the meter (the newsroom's, not yet wired to this site), a form, any customer's private details, and the derived half of the review folder. Every number on a page is counted from a file.
